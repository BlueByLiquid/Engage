How this guide was assembled

Editorial picks and descriptions were weighed against the multi-list consensus, episode-by-episode criticism, and continuity-focused viewing recommendations. This is an opinionated guide, not a claim that every fan agrees. Episode numbering treats the two-hour pilot and finale as one entry each; some streaming services split them, so subsequent episode numbers may differ.
